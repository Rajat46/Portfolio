About Me • Career Experience

About.

Over a decade of experience crafting thoughtful digital experiences, scaling design systems, and directing cross-functional teams.

Explore Maximum to Design Minimum
My name is Rajat Bhandari, and I'm a Senior Lead UX Designer and Acting Design Manager with over a decade of experience crafting thoughtful, user-centered solutions and leading projects that balance enterprise strategy with design craft. With a focus on strategic thinking, I excel at guiding cross-functional teams through product iteration, scaling design systems, and directing design organizations. Based in Bengaluru, India, operating across a dual mandate leading 23 designers at UST while deployed as Principal Product Designer at Dell Technologies.
2022 – Present
UST × Dell Technologies, Senior Lead UX Designer & Acting Design Manager.
Bengaluru, India

Operating as Organizational Design Manager leading people and capability strategy for 23 designers at UST, while concurrently serving as embedded Principal Product Designer driving core platform architecture at Dell Technologies. Owns hiring, career pathing, and performance calibration across 4 Leads, 10 Seniors, and 9 Mids.

2019 – 2022
Mirra Healthcare, UX Lead.
Bengaluru, India

Directed a 10-person design team owning the digital product portfolio across the US Healthcare ecosystem (Payer, Provider, Patient). Led 0-to-1 replacement of manual spreadsheet operations with automated enterprise portals, delivering a 90% efficiency improvement across patient census tracking and architecting a unified HIPAA-compliant design system.

2018 – 2019
K-Arogia Advisory Services, UX & UI Designer.
Bengaluru, India

Conceptualized and shipped end-to-end operational software for hospital networks as sole designer, including clinical analytics dashboards and the "3-2-9" post-discharge care management tool.

2018
Kansoftware, UX & UI Designer.
Udaipur, India

Built native client UI for the Jival mobile app in Udaipur, establishing strong practical engineering foundations across mobile component lifecycles, layout rendering performance, and user interaction mechanics.

2016 – 2018
Obbserv Online Services, Android Developer to Founding UI/UX Designer.
Udaipur, India

Served as the first UX designer for GoodDot (India's pioneering plant-based meat alternative brand), architecting v1.0 of the consumer e-commerce web platform and conversion checkout funnel from the ground up. Transitioned from Android engineering into full-stack product design, delivering production applications (awarded Most Productive Team Award).
